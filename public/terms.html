What the app does

It reads workout figures and a game title from a photo you supply, asks you to confirm them, and stores the result. Optionally it also records the workout in your Google Health account.

The numbers are estimates

Workout figures are read from a photograph by an automated system and may be wrong. That is why the app asks you to check every value before saving. Floors are calculated from steps at a fixed sixteen steps per floor, which is an approximation and will not match every machine exactly. Do not rely on anything here for medical purposes.
