Privacy Policy

StairGamer · last updated 19 September 2026

StairGamer is a personal fitness-logging app. You photograph the results screen of a stair machine after a workout; the app reads the numbers and the game you were playing off the photo, and stores the result so you can look back at it later.

This policy describes exactly what the app collects, where it goes, and who else sees it.

What is collected

DataWhy
The photo you submitRead for workout figures; kept so you can see the original later
The photo's capture date (EXIF)So the workout is filed on the day it happened, not the day you uploaded it
Steps, duration, game nameThe workout record itself
Your Google account id and emailSign-in, and to keep your workouts separate from anyone else's

There is no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. No cookies are set beyond those Google Sign-In requires to keep you signed in.

Who else sees your data

Anthropic. Your photo is sent to Anthropic's Claude API, which reads the numbers and identifies the game. This is the only way the photo leaves Google's infrastructure. Anthropic processes it to return that result. See Anthropic's privacy policy.

Google. Photos and workout records are stored in Firebase (Google Cloud), in the United States. If — and only if — you tick Log this workout in Google Health, the app writes the step count, duration and start time of that workout to your own Google Health account. Nothing is written to Google Health unless you tick that box and grant permission, and you can revoke that permission at any time from your Google account permissions.

Your data is never sold, never shared with advertisers, and never given to anyone else.

How long it is kept

  • A photo you upload but never confirm is deleted automatically within about a day.
  • Confirmed workouts and their photos are kept until you ask for them to be deleted.

Your choices

  • Google Health logging is off by default and only happens when you tick the box.
  • You can revoke Google Health access at any time; the app will simply stop logging there.
  • You can request a copy of your data, or its deletion, by contacting the address below.

Contact

StairGamer is run by one person, not a company. For anything to do with your data, including deletion, write to stairgamer.exe@gmail.com.
